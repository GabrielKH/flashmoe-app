## Model

Qwen3.6-35B-A3B is made by the Qwen team at Alibaba Cloud and released under the Apache License 2.0. flashmoe downloads it from Hugging Face (`mlx-community/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-4bit`, and the multi-token-prediction head from `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B`) and converts it on your Mac. The weights are not included in or redistributed with flashmoe.
